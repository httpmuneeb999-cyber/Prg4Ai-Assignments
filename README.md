## Detailed Script Descriptions

### 1. Student Marks & Grade Calculator (`01.py`)
* **Functionality:** Accepts name, roll number, and marks for three subjects (Python, AI, and Maths) for 5 students[cite: 4].
* **Features:** 
  * Calculates total marks and percentage[cite: 4].
  * Assigns letter grades (A, B, C, D, F) based on performance thresholds[cite: 4].
  * Identifies and highlights the student with the highest percentage[cite: 4].
  * Exports formatted records to `results.txt`[cite: 4].

### 2. Sentence & Text Analyzer (`02.py`)
* **Functionality:** Takes a custom sentence input from the user and performs comprehensive text analysis[cite: 5].
* **Features:** 
  * Counts total words and characters[cite: 5].
  * Identifies the longest and shortest words[cite: 5].
  * Counts total vowels (`a, e, i, o, u`)[cite: 5].
  * Extracts unique words and sorts them alphabetically[cite: 5].
  * Saves the analysis summary into `word_analysis.txt`[cite: 5].

### 3. Shopping Cart & Bill Generator (`03.py`)
* **Functionality:** Manages a shopping list for 5 products, tracking names, prices, and quantities[cite: 1].
* **Features:** 
  * Computes item-wise and total bill amounts[cite: 1].
  * Applies a **10% discount** automatically if the total bill exceeds 10,000[cite: 1].
  * Identifies the most expensive product purchased[cite: 1].
  * Generates an itemized receipt and saves it to `bill.txt`[cite: 1].

### 4. Interactive Arithmetic Calculator (`04.py`)
* **Functionality:** Provides a command-line interface to perform basic mathematical operations[cite: 2].
* **Operations Supported:** 
  * Addition (`+`)[cite: 2]
  * Subtraction (`-`)[cite: 2]
  * Multiplication (`*`)[cite: 2]
  * Division (`/`)[cite: 2]
  * Modulus (`%`)[cite: 2]
* **Features:** Runs inside a continuous `while` loop allowing users to perform multiple calculations sequentially[cite: 2].

### 5. Restaurant Menu Ordering System (`05.py`)
* **Functionality:** Simulates a digital food menu with pre-defined items and prices[cite: 3].
* **Features:** 
  * Displays a menu list with item numbers[cite: 3].
  * Allows users to select items by entering their respective numbers and specifying quantities[cite: 3].
  * Accumulates the final running bill until the user enters `0` to checkout[cite: 3].
  * Logs the final order summary into `orders.txt`[cite: 3].
